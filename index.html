From an empty bay to a routed driver, in four steps.

ParkingAI doesn't guess — it watches, learns, and predicts, then puts that prediction directly in front of the driver who needs it.

1

Sensors watch every bay

Ultrasonic sensors and existing CCTV feeds detect whether each space is occupied, updated in real time.

2

Data streams to the cloud

Every reading is timestamped and pushed into a central pipeline the moment it happens.

3

The model looks ahead

A prediction engine blends live readings with historical patterns to forecast what opens up next.

4

Drivers get routed there

The app shows the most likely open spot on a live map and guides the driver straight to it.

Built around one question: where can I park right now?

Every feature exists to answer that question faster, or to help operators plan for the answer.
